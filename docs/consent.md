# Recording consent

The foundation preview records no audio. Its start action is an explicitly labeled simulation, and the sample meeting is fictional.

When real capture is implemented, inform participants and obtain whatever consent applicable law and organizational policy require before recording. A visible indicator or a local OS permission prompt is not participant consent. System capture can include unrelated application audio; headphones do not change recording obligations.

Do not remove the preview warning or rename simulated results as actual recordings until capture has passed its platform and privacy gates. Real recording must ship with a visible indicator, a working pause control that excludes paused audio from storage, and an honest failure state.
